## Reporting a Vulnerability

Open a new issue in this repository.

Label it as bug and vulnerability; I'm gonna look at it within a day or two.
If the concern is justified it's probably gonna take me a couple of days to get it sorted out, might sporadically take up to a week.
If the concern is not justified I'll still respond and then close the issue.
